how grail works

Collect

gems

Each season, we release a host of world-class cards into chests, ready for our users to find. Each card is split into gems, allowing you to own part of these iconic cards with other members of the community.

Customize Your

avatar

And Join The Community

The biggest part of Grail is our community; a friendly group of like-minded collectibles lovers. Customise your avatar using cosmetics, and chat with other users about the hobby you love.

Trade and Sell

your

Gems and Cosmetics

We empower our users to build collections in the way they want to. Trade or sell your gems with the community, and build a collection you’re proud of.

All Cards Are Distributed at the End of the

Season

At the end of each season, each gem holder votes on what they’d like to do with their card. If holders choose Randomise, one lucky gem holder takes full ownership of the card.

If Auction is chosen, we sell the card with one of our auction partners and transfer all proceeds to the gem holders.

We then start the next season, with a whole new host of world-class cards.
