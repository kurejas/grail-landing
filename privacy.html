Privacy Policy

This policy explains what we collect when you use Grail — the app and this website — why we collect it, and the choices you have. We only ask for what we need to run Grail.

Last updated 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

Grail (“Grail”, “we”, “us”) runs the Grail app and website and is responsible for the personal information described here.

2. What we collect on this website

When you sign up for early access we collect your name and email address. Along with them we record the time of your sign-up, the page you sent it from and your IP address. The website does not use cookies or analytics.

3. What we collect in the app

When you create an account we collect the details you give us, such as your name and email address, together with your activity in Grail: the gems you hold, your votes, trades and purchases, and the technical information your device sends us so the app can work.

4. How we use it

We use your information to create and run your account, send you the founding member rewards and updates you signed up for, run each season’s votes, trades and auctions, and keep Grail safe for the community.

5. Fonts

This website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address. We do not send Google anything else.

6. Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We share it only with the service providers who help us run Grail, such as hosting, email and payments, with our auction partners when a card you hold goes to auction, and when the law requires us to.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is open, or for as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it for. Sign-up details are kept until Grail launches, unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. We will respond within the time the law allows.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so please keep your login details safe.

10. Children

Grail is for people aged 18 or over, or the age of majority where you live. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Get in touch through Help in the Grail app.
